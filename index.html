CAREER

You start with a BAVARIS SP 320 and a few thousand dollars. Drive to the glowing APEX GARAGE to install upgrades, paint your car and swap rides.

Race markers are scattered across the city. Finish 1st to bank the big payout and unlock new challenges. Speed traps and drift zones pay cash in free roam.

Time flows from day to night. The city's neon comes alive after sunset — visibility drops, so use your headlights.
